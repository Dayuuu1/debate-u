## Prompts finales

### Fondo oscuro

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: premium institutional event background, wide 16:9 landscape. Create a polished restrained backdrop for a rector election debate at an Amazonian Peruvian university, using a deep burgundy charcoal base (#210d1b), vivid fuchsia (#d60050) restrained gradient lighting, very fine subtle diagonal satin planes and a thin understated geometric diamond textile-inspired band running ONLY along the far right edge. The central 75% and entire left area are calm, nearly uniform dark wine negative space for later UI text and candidate photos. Sophisticated academic broadcast design, flat front-facing digital background, matte finish, crisp subtle texture, not a scene or a mockup. All details quiet and concentrated at right edge and lower right corner. No letters, no text, no numerals, no logos, no emblems, no shields, no people, no leaves, no landscapes, no gold, no glitter. Wide landscape 16:9, high resolution. The background must work cropped vertically anchored at the right edge.

### Fondo claro

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: premium institutional event background, wide 16:9 landscape. Create a polished minimalist light backdrop for a rector election debate at an Amazonian Peruvian university: warm off-white (#fff9fc), extremely subtle pale blush, fine pearl matte paper surface, very faint diagonal satin planes. A restrained vivid fuchsia (#d60050) ribbon accent and thin geometric diamond textile-inspired band ONLY at the far right edge and bottom right corner, hints of charcoal outlines within the tiny geometric border. Center 75% and entire left area nearly uniform warm white negative space for live UI text and candidate photos. Sophisticated academic broadcast design, flat front-facing digital background, not a scene or mockup. Beautiful but understated, lots of clean white. No letters, no text, no numerals, no logos, no emblems, no shields, no people, no leaves, no landscapes, no gold, no glitter. Wide landscape 16:9, high resolution. Must work cropped vertically anchored at right edge.
